II Concurso de Fotografia da Biodiversidade de Vila Real

September 24, 2014

Back in August 2014, I participated in the II Vila Real Biodiversity Photography Contest. The winners, classified in 1st, 2nd and 3rd place and honourable mention of each category were later exhibited, on September 12th, at 21:30, at the Museum of Sound and Image located in the building of the Teatro de Vila Real.

Even tho I didn’t win anything, being an element in the honourable mention section was still a pleasure and it secured my first photo in an exhibition!

The works were later published in “BIODIVERSIDADE DE VILA REAL : NOVA ABORDAGEM FOTOGRÁFICA”, ISBN: 978-989-8653-26-0”.
